### 七、Mike 读到一篇介绍椰子的文章，和大家分享。（10 分）

Coconuts are amazing fruit that grow on coconut trees. These trees are tall and can grow up to thirty meters high! They have long, green leaves and grow best in warm places like beaches in Hainan.

The coconut has a hard, brown shell outside, but inside, it holds sweet water called coconut water and soft, white flesh（果肉）called coconut meat. People use coconuts in many ways:

- Coconut water is a healthy drink.
- Coconut meat can be eaten or used to make coconut milk and oil.
- The shell can be turned into bowls or crafts.
- The leaves are used to make roofs or baskets.

Coconut trees are very useful, and people call them the “Tree of Life”, because every part of the tree can be used.
