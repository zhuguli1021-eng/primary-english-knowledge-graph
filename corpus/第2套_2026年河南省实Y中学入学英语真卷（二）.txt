Ⅳ. 阅读理解。（共 5 分）
Henry is a cute corgi (柯基). He is six years old. To celebrate (庆祝) Henry's being a police dog for one year, people there have a party for him.
People buy many things for Henry. They buy a party hat, a nice black T-shirt and some food. Henry looks really cool in the hat and T-shirt. He looks very happy, too. He has a good time at the party.
Henry isn't very big, but he does many "big" things. He's always very busy with his job, but he does well in it. He helps many people. Police officers are proud of (对……感到自豪) him.
Henry likes to play ball games with people. It's interesting for him. He likes to eat vegetables and meat. He has good eating habits (习惯) because he needs to eat well to help people.
What a great dog! Do you want to have a corgi like Henry?
