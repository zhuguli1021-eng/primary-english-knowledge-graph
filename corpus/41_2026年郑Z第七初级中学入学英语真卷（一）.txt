When it comes to Chinese and Western restaurants, the differences are
big. One difference is the menus.
In Western restaurants, the menus are usually very descriptive(描述性的). They tell you what is in each
dish. You can also know if a dish is fried or steamed(蒸).
Chinese menus don’t usually tell you these things. But you can see a lot of pictures on them. Most
Western restaurant menus do not have as many pictures as Chinese ones. In some fancy(奢华的) Western
restaurants, the menus may not have any pictures at all.
The names of Chinese dishes are usually not very descriptive. Having more photos also makes the menus
more attractive to people. It can make people feel good about eating in the restaurant.
