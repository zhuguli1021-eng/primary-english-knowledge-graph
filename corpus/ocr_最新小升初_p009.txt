The Best Way to See Lakeland
TOUR A
Lake District Spectacular
The Lake District is famous for being "the most beautiful corner of England" and the
tour introduces you to just that fantastic scenery of mountains, valleys and lakes from the
south to the north. The tour includes a lake cruise, a visit to the 4,000-year-old Castlerigg
Stone Circle set high on the hills. You travel over passes, through beautiful valleys, see
waterfalls and traditional Lakeland villages. You have plenty of stops for admiring the
views and taking photographs.
This is a tour not to be missed.

TOUR B
Beatrix Potter's Favorite Countryside
The tour is the ideal way to explore the favorite places associated with Beatrix Potter.
Our journey is through the beautiful Yewdale Valley, passing Yew Tree Tam and Skelwith
Bridge making our way to Coniston Water for our lake cruise. We visit the Beatrix
Potter's Hill Top farm where she wrote many of her famous children's stories in this little
old stone house. You have the chance to go in the shop and explore her garden.
