Ⅳ. 阅读理解。（共10分）
Mr Zhang is a kind cleaner at a small school. Every day, he gets up early to start his job. He sweeps
floors and cleans classrooms. His job is humble, but his family love him very much.
Mr Zhang’s wife, Mrs Zhang, is a nice and hard-working woman. She looks after their small house and
cooks delicious meals for the family every day. Their daughter, Xiaomei, is a smart and cute girl. She loves to
share her school stories with her parents.
One evening, Xiaomei comes back home with a bad grade on her math exam. She is very unhappy and
feels like she lets her parents down. But instead of getting angry, Mr Zhang hugs（拥抱）her and tells her it is
not a big thing. He tries to help her study math. From that day on, Mr Zhang does his best to help Xiaomei.
With his help and love, Xiaomei has good grades in math and ▲______.
Mr Zhang’s story shows that love and support（支持）can make a big difference in people’s lives. He
doesn’t have a good job or much money, but he can have a happy family and enjoy wonderful family time with
much love.
