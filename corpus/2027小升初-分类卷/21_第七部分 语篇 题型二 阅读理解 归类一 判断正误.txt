Passage 19（2024 成都 XC 中学）★★★☆☆

The Double Ninth Festival is celebrated on the ninth day of the ninth lunar（阴历的）month, and it is also called the Chongyang Festival. People celebrate this festival in many different ways.

Go mountain climbing

People like to climb mountains at this festival. It is a good time for sightseeing.

Drink chrysanthemum wine

Chrysanthemum blooms in September. On the Double Ninth Festival, people appreciate chrysanthemum flowers and drink chrysanthemum wine. They believe（相信）that the wine is good for health.

Wear cornel

It is also a traditional custom to wear cornel. Cornel is a kind of plant. Women used to stick the flower in their hair or hang the cornel’s branch on windows or doors to avoid evil（避免邪恶）.

Eat Chongyang cakes

The tradition of eating Chongyang cakes in September originated from ancient times. The Chinese name for this cake is Gao. It’s a nine-layered cake which means people moving to a higher level.

Visit elderly relatives

In 1890, the Double Ninth Festival became the Seniors’ Day officially. You can visit your ancestors’ graves, or simply spend time with your elderly relatives.

（　　）1. The Double Ninth Festival is on the ninth day of September.

（　　）2. People usually climb mountains at this festival.

（　　）3. The Chongyang cake means that our life will become better and better.

（　　）4. People celebrate the Double Ninth Festival in five ways according to the passage.

（　　）5. We can spend time with our elderly relatives on the Double Ninth Festival.
