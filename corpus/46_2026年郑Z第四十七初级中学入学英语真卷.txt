Ⅰ. 阅读理解。（共20分）
A
If you are a middle school student, you will spend over eight hours a day in the school. But do you know
how to make your school life better? Here is some advice for you. Maybe it is helpful.
First of all, you should know what the most important thing is in school. Of course, it is your study. You
should listen carefully to teachers in class. You can also go to the library, if there is one in your school. It’s a
quiet place for you to study and you can read different kinds of books there.
Joining a school club can also make you relaxed. There you can know more about what you like and meet
many friends. They may have the same hobby as you. Do you have any good friends around you? You can
share good and happy things with them. That’s great. Don’t forget to help your classmates and your teachers.
When they say “thank you” with a smile, you will find that it is the best moment(瞬间) in your school life.
