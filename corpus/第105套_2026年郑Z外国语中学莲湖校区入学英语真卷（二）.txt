The government built the crying room to encourage people to cry and ask for help when they're under stress. People celebrate their birthdays every year. But do you know that flowers also have a birthday?
The Flower Festival (花朝节) is the birthday of flowers in China. It started in the Tang Dynasty (AD 618-907). People celebrate it during the second lunar (农历的) month. The dates are different in different places across China. But it is usually in March or April.
There are 12 goddesses of flowers. Each is in charge of (负责) one month of a year. On the day of the festival, people often offer incense (香) and spirits (烈酒) to the flower goddesses. They wish for good weather and a good harvest for the year.
People usually have fun outdoors for the Flower Festival. Family members go to the countryside and have a picnic near flowers. Some people may also plant some flowers.
The Flower Festival is also a festival for girls. Girls get together and make friends. They wear flowers in their hair. They put red ribbons (丝带) on trees. They also make desserts and rice cakes with flowers.
The festival is like a big birthday party for flowers. Do you want to celebrate it?
