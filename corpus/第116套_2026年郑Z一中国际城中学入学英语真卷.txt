II. 阅读理解。（共 10 分）
My name is Bill. I'm in No. 6 Middle School. I love soccer, ping-pong and basketball. I can play them well. They are easy and interesting for me. I have three soccer balls, six ping-pong balls and two basketballs. I play them with my classmates after class. But I think baseball is too difficult.
This is my friend, Kate. We are in the same school. But we are not classmates. She is crazy (疯狂的) about baseball. She has 16 baseballs, 8 baseball bats and 5 baseball hats! But she can't play baseball. She thinks it is difficult for her. She likes to watch baseball games on TV after dinner.
(    ) 1. What sport can Bill not play well?
A. Basketball.  B. Soccer.  C. Ping-pong.  D. Baseball.
(    ) 2. Bill has ________.
A. 4 soccer balls and 16 baseballs  B. 6 ping-pong balls and 5 basketballs
C. 16 baseballs and 8 baseball bats  D. 6 ping-pong balls and 3 soccer balls
(    ) 3. Kate is Bill's ________.
A. classmate  B. sister  C. friend  D. cousin
(    ) 4. What does Kate like to do after dinner?
A. She likes to watch baseball games on TV.  B. She likes to watch basketball games on TV.
C. She likes to play baseball with her friends.  D. She likes to play basketball with her friends.
(    ) 5. Which of the following is NOT true?
A. Kate is in No. 6 Middle School.  B. Bill plays basketball with Kate every day.
C. Bill likes to play ping-pong.  D. Kate loves baseball, but she can't play it.
