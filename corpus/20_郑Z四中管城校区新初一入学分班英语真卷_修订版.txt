IV. 阅读理解。(共30分)
A
Emily is a student at a middle school. She enjoys outdoor activities, and her favorite activity is flying kites. She has many colorful kites, and each kite has a special story behind it.
On a sunny afternoon, Emily takes her favorite kite, a red one from her grandfather, to the park. The kite flies high in the sky, and Emily has a great time. However, a strong wind carries it away.
She spends hours looking for her lost kite, but she can’t find it anywhere. Her eyes fill with tears, and she feels sad for a long time.
As time goes on, Emily realizes that the kite itself is not the most important thing. What she misses most are the days when she and her grandfather flew the kite together.
Emily decides to make new memories(记忆) with her grandfather. She spends time with him, listening to his stories and learning from him. Their relationship(关系) becomes stronger. Emily finds that the love they share is more precious(珍贵的) than anything.

①Friends are really important in our daily life. They are like the warm sunshine that makes our days better.
②Friends bring us lots of fun. We can play games, like tag or hide-and-seek. We also have a great time chatting and sharing our little secrets. For example, we talk about our favorite toys or candies. When we are sad, friends will comfort(安慰) us. If we fall and hurt ourselves, they will help us up and say kind words.
③Friends also help us learn. If friends are good at reading, they can read with us and make reading more fun. We can learn different ideas from our friends, too. Sometimes, a friend may have a new way to solve a problem that we never thought of.
④In short, friends are very special in our daily life. They give us happiness, care, and help us grow. We should value our friendship. With it, our daily life becomes more wonderful and meaningful. Let’s enjoy the time with our friends every day!
