Ⅲ. 阅读理解。（共10分）
Yuan Longping is a great scientist. He is the “father of hybrid rice” in China. In 1930, he was born（出生）in Beijing. In 1939, his family went to Chongqing and lived there. He studied in a university there from 1949 to 1953. Then he went to Hunan and worked as a teacher from 1953 to 1970.
He had many hobbies. He liked playing the violin and swimming. He could speak English well. When he was young, he saw lots of Chinese people starving. People couldn’t eat rice every day. Sometimes they ate grass or leaves! He wanted to help them.
In 1960, he found（发现）a special rice plant—hybrid rice. He studied it for a few years. In 1973, he planted the rice successfully（成功地）. There’s more and more rice, and hybrid rice can grow in other countries, too. Yuan Longping helped China and he also helped the world.
