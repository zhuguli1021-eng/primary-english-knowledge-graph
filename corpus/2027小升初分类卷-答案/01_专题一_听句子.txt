【听力材料】

I. 听句子，选出你所听到的内容。

1. I will take a trip tomorrow.

2. What are you going to do this evening?

3. Do you often play the piano on Sundays?

4. Where is your brother going tonight?

5. They are going to the cinema by taxi.

II. 听句子，判断正误，正确的用“T”表示，错误的用“F”表示。

1. You can’t park here.

2. Today is July 1st.

3. The girl is watching TV now.

4. The panda is so cute.

5. The boy took a trip to the beach.

III. 判断下列句子与所听句子是（T）否（F）相同。

1. Jenny does her homework on Saturdays.

2. Tom is very happy today.

3. I will go to Xinjiang on my holiday.

4. I’m going to play the *pipa*.

5. I feel angry. I should count to ten.

IV. 听句子，给下列图片排序。

1. There are many tall buildings in Ningbo.

2. Alice can play the piano well.

3. People in China eat *tangyuan* on the Lantern Festival.

4. We can build a snowman in winter.

5. I want to eat noodles in the restaurant.

V. 听句子，给下列句子排序。

1. My uncle is a fisherman.

2. He sees lots of fish every day.

3. He works on a boat. He goes to work by bike.

4. He works very hard and stays healthy.

5. We should stay healthy, too.

VI. 根据听到的内容选择恰当的应答句，将字母标号填入括号内。

1. Thank you for helping me with my maths.

2. Can I borrow your crayons?

3. Is this your ruler?

4. Where is Wenchuan?

5. Are you tired?

6. How many rulers do you have?

7. Which class is David in?

8. How much are these apples?

9. How about this blue one?

10. May I speak to your mother?

VII. 听录音，选出你听到的句子。

1. The girl can’t walk or speak.

2. Her mother can swim.

3. You can go to school by bike.

右栏：

4. I love rabbits. They are so cute.

5. Jack is listening to music in the living room.

VIII. 听句选图。请选出你听到的句子所代表的图片。

1. Soon the fifth pea grew into a plant with some green leaves.

2. We can use waste paper to make paper planes.

3. Jill wants to make a crown for her cousin.

4. Students will study online at home in the future.

5. In autumn, farmers get the crops in.

IX. 听句子，补全所缺单词。

1. He likes drawing pictures.

2. My mother bought presents for me on my twelfth birthday.

3. I had fun with my brother last summer holiday.

4. Wednesday is the fourth day of the week.

5. My uncle is 32 years old. My father is 2 years younger than him.

X. 选出你所听到的句子的汉语意思。

1. He had a lot of sweet food.

2. She wants to be a writer.

3. I visited my grandparents last weekend.

4. Amy is drawing a picture.

5. They live in Australia.

XI. 写出你所听到的句子。

1. I washed my clothes last weekend.

2. Is the monkey stronger than the elephant?

3. I usually go to school by bus.

4. Dancing makes me happy and beautiful.

5. I give my sister a little flag.
