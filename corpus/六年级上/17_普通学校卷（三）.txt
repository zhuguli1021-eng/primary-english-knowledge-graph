六、你是否也为该怎样使用零花钱而苦恼呢？我们一起来看一下 Dongdong 的做法吧！（10 分）

I’m Dongdong. I get some pocket money from my parents every month. I don’t spend it all at once. I have a good plan for my money.

First, I save some money every month. I want to buy a nice bike, so I put money in my piggy bank. Saving little by little helps me reach my dream.

Second, I use some money for my hobbies. I love swimming, so I pay for my swimming lessons. I also pay for my haircuts when I need a tidy look.

Third, I use a little money to buy small gifts for my family on their birthdays. It makes them happy, and I feel proud.

I think managing pocket money teaches me to be careful. I learn to save, spend wisely, and love my family.
