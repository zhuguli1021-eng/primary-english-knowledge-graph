### 七、上一个春节，Chen Jie 一家是如何过的？（10 分）

The Spring Festival is a very important festival in China. My family and I were busy preparing for it. My dad and I cleaned the house to sweep away bad luck. My mother cooked delicious food. My sister and I pasted *fu* on the doors.

*Fu* means good luck is coming. In the evening, we sat together and waited for the Spring Festival Gala on TV. We ate the delicious food. When it was nearly 12 o’clock, we counted down to the new year excitedly. The next morning, my sister and I woke up early. We dressed in red new clothes.

We all had a wonderful time during the Spring Festival.
