First, __1__ the plastic we use—bring a cloth bag when we go shopping and don’t buy too many single-use things. Second, __2__ old bottles as flower pots for small plants, and we can also use old boxes to keep our toys. Third, __3__ paper, cans and plastic bottles by putting them into the right recycling bins. Let’s start our green life now, and our Earth will be happier!
