七、你对我们所在的太阳系了解多少？ （10 分）

The solar system is our home in space. Let’s learn some fun facts about it!

The closest planet to the Sun is Mercury, but it is not the hottest. The hottest planet is Venus, with a temperature of about 467°C. Jupiter, the biggest planet, has a huge storm called the Great Red Spot. It may be more than 300 years old and is wider than Earth. Neptune has the fastest wind in the solar system—its winds can reach over 2,000 kilometres per hour. Saturn has over ten moons, and scientists are still looking for more.

On the Moon, there are footprints from astronauts. They can stay there for thousands of years because there is no air or water to wash them away. There are also thousands of pieces of space junk floating around Earth. These are man-made objects left in space, and their number is growing.
