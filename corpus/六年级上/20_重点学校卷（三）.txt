七、阅读理解。（10 分）

Nowadays, green energy is very important for our lives. It is clean and does not pollute the environment. The most common kinds are solar power, wind power and water power.

We can use sunlight to make electricity. Solar panels on the houses and buildings take energy from the Sun. Wind turns large wind turbines to make electricity, too. When water runs down from high places, it can also make power. These ways all help us get clean electricity.

However, clean energy is not enough. We still need to save energy in our daily life. We should turn off lights and TVs when we leave the room. We can use LED lights instead of old ones. It’s good to walk or ride bikes for short trips.

If we use clean energy well and save electricity, our world will become more beautiful and healthier.
