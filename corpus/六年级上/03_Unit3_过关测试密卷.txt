### 七、“健康生活”征文活动正在开展。Mike 写了一篇文章投稿。（10 分）

Do you want to live a healthy life? A healthy lifestyle includes body, mind and social parts.

First, 1.

Don’t stay up late. Eat healthy food like vegetables and fruit, and drink enough water every day.

You should also exercise often, such as running or walking. Washing hands before meals is important too.

Second, 2.

When you feel sad, think about happy things or listen to soft（轻柔的）music. You can talk to your friends or family. Doing things you love, like drawing, also helps you. Taking a deep breath can calm you down when you are stressed（有压力的）.

Finally, 3.

Join clubs and teams to make new friends. Help others in need and share your happiness（幸福）.

Talking with your family every day will keep you close.

A healthy lifestyle makes you strong and happy. Let’s try it!
