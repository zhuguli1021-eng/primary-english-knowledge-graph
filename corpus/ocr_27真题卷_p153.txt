The Mystery of Migratory Birds
Every year, millions of birds fly thousands of kilometres between their breeding (li) and wintering
grounds. This incredible journey is called migration. Scientists have been studying how birds navigate such
long distances, and their discoveries are fascinating.
How Do Birds Find Their Way?
Birds use several methods to navigate:
1. The Sun and Stars: Many birds rely on the position of the sun during the day and the stars at night.
2. Earth's Magnetic Field: Some birds can sense the Earth's magnetic field, which helps them determine
direction.
3. Landmarks: Mountains, rivers, and coastlines serve as natural " signposts" for birds.
Challenges During Migration
Migration is dangerous. Birds face threats like:
• Bad weather: Storms can blow them off course.
• Predators: Hawks and other animals hunt them.
• Human activities: Tall buildings, pollution, and deforestation (#1k) make their journey harder.
