Ⅱ. 阅读理解。（共 20 分）
A
Every country has special celebrations to mark (纪念) transitions (转变) in life. Here are three important days for young people in different countries.
Do you remember your first day in school? Many children are afraid. But in Germany, things are different. Their first day in school, when they are six, is a big celebration. All of the kids have new clothes, and their parents give them a big colourful cone (圆锥筒) full of sweets and small presents. Parents take pictures of their children with the cone. Then they meet their new teachers and classmates, and they sing songs and play games.
Girls in Mexico and other countries in Latin America are very excited about their fifteenth birthday. For them, it means the transition from girls to young women, and there is a special celebration called the Quince Años. The girl gets a beautiful dress. In the evening, there is a large party in a restaurant, with a big cake in the same colour as the girl's dress. People enjoy music and dancing until very late at night.
For young people in Japan, their twentieth birthday is very important, because that is when they become adults. There is a national holiday called Coming-of-Age Day to celebrate this. On the second Monday in January, each city has a celebration for these new adults. They all go to the City Hall to listen to talks and get a gift from the town's mayor (镇长). Everyone wears new clothes, and their families take lots of photos.
