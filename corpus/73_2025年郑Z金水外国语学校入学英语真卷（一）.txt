Ⅱ. 阅读理解。（共5分）
Nowadays, school buses are popular in China. Many students take a school bus to school. It looks like a
tourist bus. The underground and common(普通的) buses often take much time. But school buses can save a
lot of time. How do children go to school in other countries?
In the USA, yellow school buses are a common way for children to get to school. There are about 180,000
school buses in the country. But why are the school buses all yellow? Yellow became the colour of school buses
in 1939. American educator(教育家) Frank Cyr first suggested(建议) it. The colour on the buses helped
warn other cars: be careful. Black letters on a yellow bus are also easy to see in the early morning.
However, there are not many school buses in the UK. Students from public schools usually take common
buses or the underground to school. In Japan, schools are small in cities. So there is no need to use school
buses. Children usually walk to school.
(   )1. A lot of students in China take ______ to school.
A. the underground               B. common buses
C. school buses                  D. a special car
(   )2. The underlined word “warn” in Paragraph 2 means “______” in Chinese.
A. 警示                          B. 照亮
C. 敲打                          D. 支持
(   )3. ______ students often go to school on foot.
A. Chinese                       B. Japanese
C. American                      D. English
(   )4. From the passage, we know ______.
A. the school bus in the USA looks like the underground
B. black letters on a yellow bus are easy to see in the early morning
C. many students in the UK take school buses to school
D. school buses are popular in Japan
(   )5. The best title of the passage is “______”.
A. How Do Students Go Back Home from School in the World?
B. Why Are School Buses Popular in China and America?
C. How Do Students Go to School in Different Countries?
D. Why Are School Buses Yellow in the USA?
Ⅲ. 阅读短文，判断句子正(T)误(F)。（共5分）
Confucius is one of the greatest teachers in Chinese history. He was born in 551 BC（公元前）in the
Kingdom of Lu, in today’s Shandong Province. When he was young, he and his mother had a hard life. At the
age of fifteen, he began to learn music, and he did well in it. Then he went on learning other subjects. When
he was thirty, he became a teacher. He started his own school. He believed everyone should have a chance to
get education（教育）whether（无论）they were rich or poor. He had about 3,000 students and many of them
became famous.
Chinese people see Confucius as the greatest thinker and also the greatest teacher. His most important
ideas are kindness and good manners（行为）. He said young people should take care of the old. People should
stop thinking of themselves and work for others. His ideas are around in people’s everyday life. Today people
can still hear them, and they go far into Southeast Asia（东南亚）.
(   )1. Confucius was born in 551 BC.
(   )2. Confucius was good at teaching when he was fifteen.
(   )3. As a teacher, Confucius thought everyone should get education.
(   )4. Only a few Confucius’ students became famous.
(   )5. The underlined word “they” refers to（指的是）Confucius and his students.
