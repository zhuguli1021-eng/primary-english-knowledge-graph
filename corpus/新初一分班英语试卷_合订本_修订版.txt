Emily is a student at a middle school. She enjoys outdoor activities, and her favorite activity is flying kites. She has many colorful kites, and each kite has a special story behind it.
On a sunny afternoon, Emily takes her favorite kite, a red one from her grandfather, to the park. The kite flies high in the sky, and Emily has a great time. However, a strong wind carries it away.
She spends hours looking for her lost kite, but she can’t find it anywhere. Her eyes fill with tears, and she feels sad for a long time.

Emily decides to make new memories(记忆) with her grandfather. She spends time with him, listening to his stories and learning from him. Their relationship(关系) becomes stronger. Emily finds that the love they share is more precious(珍贵的) than anything.
