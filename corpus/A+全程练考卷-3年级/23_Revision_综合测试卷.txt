六、阅读短文，连线。（15 分）

Hello, my name is Sarah. I have many toys. Look, I have five dogs. They are black.

I have two bears. They are blue. I have seven monkeys. They are brown. I have ten birds.

They are green. I have one elephant. It’s white. I have six ducks. They are yellow.

I like them all.

1. five　　a. bird　　A. black

2. ten　　b. bear　　B. green

3. two　　c. dog　　C. blue

4. six　　d. elephant　　D. brown

5. one　　e. monkey　　E. yellow

6. seven　　f. duck　　F. white

选词填空，补全句子。

How many ______ (duck; ducks) ?

解析：“how many” 意为“多少”，用来询问可数名词的数量，后面跟可数名词复数形式。

答案：ducks
