Ⅲ. 阅读理解。（共20分）
A
An old woman is sitting on the sofa in her house with her daughter. Just
then, a swallow（燕子）stops near them. “What’s that?” asks the mother. “It’s a swallow,” answers
the daughter. “What’s that?” asks the mother again after some minutes. “Mother, I just told you. It’s a
swallow,” answers the daughter again.
“What’s that?” asks the mother a third time（次）. “Why do you ask me the same question again and
again? I have told you so many times ‘IT IS A SWALLOW’!” shouts the daughter.
After some minutes, the mother goes to her room and comes back with an old diary. She asks her
daughter to read one page. The daughter reads the following words in the diary. Today my 4-year-old daughter
is sitting with me on the sofa. When a swallow is standing near us, she asks me 24 times what it is, and, 24
times, I answer, “It’s a swallow.” I hug her warmly every time she asks me the same question, again and again.
When your parents get old, do not look on them as a burden, but be nice to them. Remember they are
the people who always love you. Love your parents, please!
