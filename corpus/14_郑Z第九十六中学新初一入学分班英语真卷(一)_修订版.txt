VI. 阅读理解。(共15分)
A
I'm Wang Lin. I'm a student in Grade 6. I was not good at English before. Our English teacher, Mr Zhao, encouraged(鼓励)me and told me good ways to learn English. I took my teacher's advice and studied hard. So I'm good at English now.
We had an English class yesterday afternoon. There were many teachers in the classroom. They came from different schools. A woman teacher was next to me. When I couldn't read the new words, I asked my group leader(组长)for help. Then I stood up and read the new words for the whole class. After class, the woman teacher next to me gave me five and said, "Super Boy." I believe(相信)I will be better and better at English! Thanks to my group leader, my English teacher and my school!
