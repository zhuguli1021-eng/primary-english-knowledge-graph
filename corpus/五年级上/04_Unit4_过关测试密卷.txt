### 七、五（二）班就“健康生活”为主题展开了一次激烈的讨论。下面是班长在讨论会结束后做的总结。（12 分）

Everyone wants to be healthy.But how can you stay healthy? Here are some good habits to help you.

1. ______

Eating healthy food is very important. You should eat more fruits and vegetables, such as apples, bananas, carrots and broccoli. Drink milk and eat eggs for strong bones. Don’t eat too much candy or fast food because they are not good for your body.

2. ______

Water is very important for your body. You should drink at least 6 glasses of water every day. It helps you stay active and keeps your body clean.

3. ______

Playing sports or exercising makes your body strong. You can run, swim, or play basketball for 30 minutes every day. Exercise also helps you sleep better at night.

4. ______

Children need about 9~10 hours of sleep every night. If you don’t sleep enough, you will feel tired and can’t study well.

Wash your hands

Always wash your hands before eating and after using the toilet. This helps stop germs（细菌）from making you sick.
