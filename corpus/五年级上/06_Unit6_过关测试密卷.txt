### 七、天气预报说明天会有大暴雨，我们该如何应对这样的天气呢？（10 分）

Heavy rain is fun, but it can also be 1 . Here are some tips to stay safe on heavy rainy days.

• Stay indoors if possible

If the rain is very heavy, it’s safer to stay inside. Keep the windows closed. Don’t go outside unless necessary.

•

If you must go out, wear a 2 and waterproof（防水的）shoes. An umbrella can help, but be careful of strong winds.

• Be careful on the road

Walk slowly and watch your step（脚步）. Roads can be slippery（滑的）. If you see deep water, do not walk or play in it—it could be dangerous.

• Stay away from rivers and drains（下水道）

Heavy rain can make rivers rise quickly. Never play near flooded areas.

• Listen to weather warnings

Always check the weather report before going out. If there is a storm warning, stay at home.
