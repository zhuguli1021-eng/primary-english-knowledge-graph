The park is big and beautiful. There is a big forest. Many flowers are in the forest. There is a river too. Alice is excited. She likes playing in the water. But her mother stops her play in the water. It’s dangerous. Alice is a little sad.

There is a big lake next to the forest. There are many people in the lake. It’s a safe place to play in the water. Alice is excited again.

When they come back home, her mother doesn’t feel well. Alice is worried. She cooks dinner for her mother. Her mother gets well soon.
