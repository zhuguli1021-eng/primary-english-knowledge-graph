When people get sick, they need to see a doctor. Trees also need a doctor when they're sick. I'm the "tree doctor" for the ancient trees in Ditan Park.
Trees over 100 years old are called ancient trees. There are 176 ancient trees in Ditan Park, and many of them are over 300 years old!
Just like people, older trees get sick more easily, and helping them can be hard. Sometimes, their trunks rot(腐烂) and wobble. To help them stand, we build a "crutch". Trunks might be eaten, so we clean their wounds(伤口) and put medicine on them. If their roots underground can’t breathe well, we make air holes in the soil to help them.
The park keeps a special file(档案) for every old tree. Sick trees even have their own “patient files” like in a hospital. I often visit them to check how they’re doing. I walk for tens of thousands of steps each day to
take care of all my tree friends!
Tips
Some things we do might hurt old trees.
★ If you draw or carve (雕刻) words on a tree, you hurt its "skin".
★ Digging for vegetables or flowers near old trees might cut their roots underground.
★ When you camp under a tree, your trash might cover its air holes.
★ Fences (围栏) protect trees. Never climb or break them.
