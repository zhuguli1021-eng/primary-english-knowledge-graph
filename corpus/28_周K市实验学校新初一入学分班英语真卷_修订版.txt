VIII. 阅读理解。(共20分)
A
In many English homes, people eat four meals a day—breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea and dinner. People have breakfast from seven to nine in the morning. They often have cereal(麦片), bread, eggs and sausages. They also drink tea or coffee. They have lunch at about one o’clock. Afternoon tea is from four to five in the afternoon. They have dinner at about half past seven in the evening. First, they have soup, and then they have meat and vegetables. After that, they eat some fruit for dinner, like apples, oranges or bananas. But some English people have their dinner in the middle of the day. Their meals are breakfast, dinner, afternoon tea and supper. All these meals are very necessary(必要的).
阅读短文，选择最佳选项。

A. three          B. four          C. five
(   )2. What time do they have lunch?
A. At about one o’clock.    B. At two o’clock.    C. At about half past seven.
根据短文内容判断正(T)误(F)。
(   )3. Many English people often have cereal, bread, eggs and sausages for breakfast.
(   )4. Afternoon tea is from four to five in the afternoon.
(   )5. All these meals aren’t necessary.
B
A sheep is drinking water by a river. A wolf (狼) comes to the sheep. The wolf wants to eat the sheep. He says to the sheep, “You make my water dirty. I can’t drink it now.” The sheep says, “I am far away from you. How can I make your water dirty?” The wolf has nothing to say. Then the wolf has another idea. He says to the sheep, “Last year you said something bad about me.” “No, I didn’t. I wasn’t born at that time!” cries the sheep. “Then it must be your dad,” the wolf says. Then he runs to the sheep and eats him.
