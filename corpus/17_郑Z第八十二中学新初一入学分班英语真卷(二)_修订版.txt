I. 单项选择。(共10分)
(   )1. —Did you ______ Beijing last holiday?
—Yes, I ______.
A. goes to; do      B. go to; did      C. went to; did
(   )2. —______ are your pants?
—My pants are Size L.
A. What size      B. What colour      C. Where
(   )3. Before, I ______ quiet. Now, I ______ active.
A. was; was      B. was; is      C. was; am
(   )4. Were there ______ tall buildings in the city?
A. a      B. some      C. any
(   )5. —______ is Chen Jie?
—She is 1.62 metres.
A. How old      B. How heavy      C. How tall
(   )6. —Where did they go over the winter holiday?
—They ______.
A. bought gifts      B. went to Datong      C. took pictures
(   )7. My shoes are size 35. Zhang Peng’s shoes are size 36. Zhang Peng’s shoes are ______ than mine.
A. bigger      B. smaller      C. heavier
(   )8. What ______ are your shoes?
A. big      B. size      C. sizes
(   )9. The schoolbag is heavier than ______.
A. my      B. me      C. mine
(   )10. I’m taller and ______ than you.
A. heavy      B. heavier      C. strong
