I. 单项选择。(共10分)

A. an; a; the  B. a; an; the  C. the; a; an  D. a; the; an

—Yes, ______.
A. he is  B. they are  C. she is  D. she does

A. or  B. and  C. but  D. so

—I will go ______.
A. shopping  B. shopping  C. shops  D. shop

A. to do homework  B. do homework  C. do homeworks  D. doing homework

A. in  B. on  C. at  D. with

II. 阅读理解。(共10分)
Hi, I’m David. I’m 11. I’m a student of Zhengzhou Primary School. There are many clubs(社团) in my school. I am in the Swimming Club because I’m good at swimming. My favourite subject is English, and I can speak English well. So I am in the English Club. I want to join the Basketball Club, but I can’t play basketball.
Jim is my good friend and we are in the same school. He thinks swimming is good for health, but he can’t swim. He likes drawing and he draws well. He is in the Art Club. He can play chess and he is in the Chess Club too. I think playing chess is very interesting, and I am a new member in it. But I can’t play chess.
(   )1. David is ______ years old.
A. twelve      B. eleven      C. fourteen      D. ten
(   )2. How many clubs does Jim join?
A. Two.      B. Three.      C. Four.      D. Five.
(   )3. What can David do?
A. He can play chess.      B. He can play basketball.    C. He can draw well.      D. He can swim well.
(   )4. Jim is in the Art Club because ______.
A. he thinks art is very interesting    B. his hobby is drawing    C. David is in the Art Club too    D. his teacher asks him to learn drawing at school
(   )5. David and Jim are both in the ______ Club.
A. Chess      B. Swimming      C. Art      D. English
III. 完形填空。(共10分)
阅读下面短文，掌握其大意，然后从每题所给的A、B、C三个选项中，选出能填入短文相应空白处的
最佳选项。
Helen is my good friend. Her father is an English teacher. He likes sports. He __1__ to school with Helen every morning. Helen’s mother is __2__ store assistant(助手). She sells __3__ at Mrs Black’s Clothes Store. After school, Helen often __4__ her mother in that store. Lots of clothes in the store are __5__ in the evening. They have jackets in many __6__—red, yellow, blue...You can __7__ them for only 50 yuan each.
They also have beautiful sweaters __8__ good prices. They also have __9__ and cheap(便宜的) socks and shoes. Do you need __10__?
(   )1. A. go B. going C. goes
(   )2. A. / B. a C. an
(   )3. A. clothes B. fruit C. vegetables
(   )4. A. helps B. wants C. needs
(   )5. A. on sale B. on the top C. on TV
(   )6. A. rooms B. colours C. schools
(   )7. A. need B. help C. buy
(   )8. A. in B. at C. for
(   )9. A. big B. short C. nice
(   )10. A. them B. us C. it
IV. 书面表达。(共10分)
假如你是张华，你的加拿大笔友Lisa最近给你发来一封电子邮件。请你根据邮件中的内容和问题，
给她回封邮件。
Dear Zhang Hua,
How are you? I want to ask you some questions. Do you have a big or small family? Who are they? What sport do you like? Who do you play it with? What food do you like? What do you like for breakfast? How about lunch and dinner? I like nice skirts and I like reading books. What about you?
Yours,
Lisa
