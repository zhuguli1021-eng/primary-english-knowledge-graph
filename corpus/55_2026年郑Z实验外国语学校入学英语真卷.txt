A
Like many other young boys, I didn’t know what I wanted to be when I was a middle school student. My
father’s advice for me was to be a teacher like him, but it seemed that I was not interested in it. My mother
wanted me to be a pianist. However, I couldn’t stand(忍受) spending all of my time in front of a piano.
I got my goal after I went to senior high school. I got a part-time job at the school radio station and I found
it was interesting. I loved to discuss school life and my favourite music with my schoolmates on the radio. I
kept doing this job during the next six years. When I left the university, I got a full-time job at the radio
station in my town. My career(事业) began. I succeeded in my program. I have a lot of fans and enjoy my
job and my life now.
So don’t just follow other people’s opinions when you have no idea about your dream job. Try to get part-
time jobs to experience and find out what you are really interested in.

B
Have you heard of dazi? Have you ever found a dazi? These days, finding a dazi is becoming popular with
young Chinese people. A dazi is a person we eat with, study with, exercise with or take a trip with, but we
haven’t known him or her before.
The dazi culture is about finding the right person to do the right activity with. Xiaoyue, a young woman
from Ningxia, has met many dazis because of her love for basketball and biking. Having a dazi not only gives
her company(陪伴) but also helps her enjoy sports more.
These dazi relationships are very different from close friendships. For close friends, we need to stay in
close contact. With a dazi, you don’t have to do so. Many dazis may not meet again after a trip or a game
together. So, many people believe having a dazi makes their social life easier and more comfortable.
Finding a dazi shows that young Chinese people want clearer boundaries（界限）in their social
relationships. Dazi will not talk or ask a lot about their personal lives. And they hope these relationships can
let them share their hobbies and interests or that they can help them to improve themselves.
Although finding a dazi has many good points, we should be careful and put our safety first.
