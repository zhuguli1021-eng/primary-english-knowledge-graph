Do you like reading books? Mr Men and Little Miss, the children’s book, is one of your best choices（选择）. There are many interesting stories in this book. Here let’s take a look at some funny persons in the stories.
Mr Tickle has a funny face and long arms. He has no hair. He can make breakfast on his bed with his long arms. What does Mr Tickle want to do? He likes tickling（使发痒）people very much. He always stands where you can’t see him, tickling you with his long arms!
Little Miss Sunshine is the happiest person in the stories. She is kind to everyone. She has a round face and a small nose. Her eyes are not big but she has a big mouth. Her best friend is Mr Happy. They want to make friends with you.
Mr Greedy is short and heavy. He has a big mouth and a big stomach. He can eat much. His legs are short and fat. He is always hungry and eats a lot. He has dreams about food at night.
