He and his wife lived in the countryside and didn’t know much about the world.
As he was buying his things, the owner of the store said, “Hey, Sam. I have something special here that
you might like. It’s called a ‘match’. You can use it to make fire. It’s very convenient(方便的)!”

store owner said. He then took out a match and struck(划) it against the side of his pants. Sam was surprised

answered, “Well, I can’t walk 12 miles into town every time I want to borrow your pants to start the fire.”
