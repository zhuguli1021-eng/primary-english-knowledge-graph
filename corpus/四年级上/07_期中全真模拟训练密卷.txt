### 六、Zhang Peng 来找 Mike，Mike 带着他逛自己所在的社区。（10 分）

Mike: My community is clean and beautiful. There is a hospital. My mother is a nurse in the hospital.

Zhang Peng: What’s your father’s job?

Mike: He is an office worker. He is busy every day.

Zhang Peng: My mother is an office worker too. And she is also busy. Look, Mike! There is a boy on the playground. Who is he?

Mike: Which boy?

Zhang Peng: He has yellow hair. He is tall and strong. He plays basketball.

Mike: Oh, his name is John. He’s my friend. Let’s go to play basketball with him.

Zhang Peng: Let’s go.
