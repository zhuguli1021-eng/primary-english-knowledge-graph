## 一、阅读理解（10 小题，每小题 1 分，共 10 分）

阅读下面的 2 篇语言材料，然后按文后要求做题。
A

The computer is fast and doesn’t make a mistake（错误）. It is right all the time. That’s what people often say when they talk about computers.

For over a quarter of a century（世纪）, engineers（工程师）have been making better computers. They make them smaller and faster. Now computers can do a lot of work. They are very useful. They are used in factories, hospitals, schools, banks and a lot of other places. They can help people. Teachers use them to teach and students use them for their studies. Computer scientists are now thinking of making the computer “think” like a man. With the help of a person, a computer can draw pictures, listen to music, talk with people, play chess and so on. Maybe computers can really think and feel one day. Do you think that people will be afraid（害怕）when they find the computer is clever enough to listen to and serve（服务）the people?

1. The computer ____________ all the time.
A. makes a mistake　B. is right　C. doesn’t work　D. can talk
2. Computers are becoming ____________.
A. bigger and faster　B. smaller and faster　C. faster and faster　D. bigger and bigger
3. You can use computers to ____________.
A. listen to music　B. draw pictures　C. play chess　D. all above
4. With the help of computers, people can do work ____________.
A. faster　B. slowly　C. worse　D. good
5. Do computers “think” like a man?
A. Yes, they do.　B. No, they don’t.　C. Yes, they like a man.　D. No, they aren’t.
B

Today is Father’s Day. The students from different countries celebrate（庆祝）it in different ways.

It’s 11: 00 a. m. in Sydney, Australia. Amy is only eight years old. She is buying fruit and vegetables in the supermarket now. She wants to buy two kilos of bananas, because bananas are her father’s favorite. She wants to make salad for her father.

It’s 10: 00 a. m. in Tokyo, Japan（日本）. The thirteen-year-old girl Yuki is in a flower shop with her friend Mary. There are many flowers of different colors in it. She wants to buy some yellow roses（玫瑰）. Her father loves yellow best. Mary wants to buy some roses for her father, too.

It’s 9: 00 a. m. in Beijing, China. Peter is in the kitchen with his little sister Helen. Their father is reading a newspaper in the living room. Today is their father’s birthday, too. The two children are making a cake for their father. It’s a pink ice-cream cake.

6. What’s Amy doing now?
A. She is washing fruit and vegetables.　B. She is making salad.　C. She is eating bananas.　D. She is doing some shopping.
7. What’s Yuki’s father’s favorite color?
A. Red.　B. White.　C. Yellow.　D. Purple.
8. What time is it in Beijing now?
A. 8: 00 a. m.　B. 9: 00 a. m.　C. 10: 00 a. m.　D. 11: 00 a. m.
9. Which is TRUE?
A. Amy is five years older than（比……年长）Yuki.　B. Yuki and Mary are in the supermarket now.　C. Mary wants to buy a yellow hat for her father.　D. Peter and Helen are making a pink ice-cream cake.
10. Which is the best title?
A. What to Buy on Father’s Day　B. Where to Go on Father’s Day　C. How to Celebrate Father’s Day　D. When to Celebrate Father’s Day

## 五、书面表达（15 分）
