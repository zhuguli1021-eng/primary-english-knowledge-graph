A. swim　B. swam　C. to swim　D. swimming

A. go　B. will go　C. have gone　D. went

A. other each　B. others　C. each other　D. the other

A. makes　B. make　C. making　D. to make

## 五、阅读理解（10 分）

A

There’s a 7-Eleven close to my house. I love the slush（冰沙）drinks in the shop. They’re quite cheap. The cups have different sizes. A small cup is only fifty cents. A large cup is one dollar（美元）. A super cup is two dollars. A mega（巨型）cup is three dollars. I usually buy a large one. When it’s very hot and I feel quite thirsty, I buy a super cup. The mega cup is too big. I never give it a go.

I get money for cleaning the floor every day after supper. If I do my job very well from Monday to Friday, Mom gives me two dollars on Saturday. When I get my money, the first thing I do is buy a slush drink. What kind of slush do I like best? The orange one. I am having my two dollars in my hand now, and I’ll go and get my slush.

1. How much is a super cup?
A. Fifty cents.　B. One dollar.　C. Two dollars.　D. Three dollars.
2. What cup does the writer often buy?
A. A small cup.　B. A large cup.　C. A super cup.　D. A mega cup.
3. What does the underlined phrase “give it a go” mean in Chinese?
A. 尝试　B. 发现　C. 担心　D. 拒绝
4. After supper, the writer ____________.
A. has a walk　B. does the cleaning　C. does the shopping　D. has a talk with his mom
5. What does the writer have in his hand now?
A. Money.　B. Slush.　C. An orange.　D. A cup.
B

A man went to buy a piece of meat. As he didn’t know how to cook meat, he asked the butcher（屠夫）to tell him the way of cooking it. The butcher told him how to cook it, “But I can’t remember your words,” the man said. “Will you please write them down for me?” The butcher was kind enough to write them down for him. The man went home happily with the meat in his hand and the note（字条）in his pocket（口袋）. A dog followed him on the way. It jumped at him, took the meat away and ran off. The man stood there, not knowing what to do. Then he laughed and said, “never mind. You don’t know how to eat it. The note is still in my pocket.”

6. The man went to buy a piece of meat in ____________.
A. restaurant　B. shop　C. butcher’s　D. chemist’s
7. Why did the man asked the butcher to tell him the way to cook meat?
A. He wasn’t good at cooking meat.　B. He can’t cook meat very well.　C. He didn’t know how to cook meat.　D. He didn’t know how to eat meat.
8. In order to（为了）remember what the butcher said, he asked the butcher ____________.
A. to say it once again　B. to teach him again
9. What happened in the end?
A. He forgot to cook the meat.　B. The dog took the meat away.　C. He taught（教）the dog how to eat the meat.　D. The note is still in his pocket.
10. What do you think of the man?
A. He was afraid of dogs.　B. He loved dogs very much.　C. He was a very foolish（愚蠢的）man.　D. He didn’t know how to do things.

## 六、作文（5 分）

随着中学体育教育越来越受到重视，你校也号召学生们积极参加体育锻炼。为此，你校英文公众号举办“My favourite sport”为主题的线上交流活动，请你根据以下要点，用英语写一篇短文，给该公众号留言。
要点如下：
1. 你最喜欢的运动是什么？
2. 你怎么喜欢上这项运动的？
3. 你从这项运动中学到了什么？
注意：
1. 短文须包含上述要点，可以适当增加细节，以使行文连贯；
2. 短文中不得出现真实姓名和学校名称；
3. 词数 70 词左右，开头已给出，不计入总词数。
Everybody has their own favourite sports.
