### 三、阅读理解（每题 1 分，共 10 分）

A

Are you learning English? If you want to learn to speak English well, there is some good advice for you:

◎ Read a book in English for an hour every day. Learn grammar(语法) and look up words in an English dictionary after reading.

◎ Listen to an audio book(有声读物) in English. You can stop it and learn what it is saying. You can speak it again after it.

◎ Spend 30 minutes in the afternoon and do some reading of the English “r” sound.

◎ Write an e-mail message in English, and look up the dictionary to be sure every word is right.

◎ Walk down the street and make English sentences in your head. Talk to yourself in English when you see something.

1. The writer thinks you can read an English book for ______ every day.
A. thirty minutes　B. an hour　C. two hours　D. three hours
2. What can you do in the afternoon according to（依据）the passage?
A. Look up words in an English dictionary.
B. Write an e-mail message in English.
C. Make English sentences in English.
D. Do some reading of English “r” sound.
3. What does the underline phrase “look up” mean?
A. 寻找　B. 借阅　C. 查阅　D. 抬头看
4. How many suggestions (建议) does the writer list（列举）?
A. four　B. five　C. three　D. six
5. What’s the best title for the passage?
A. How to Learn English Well
B. Do Some Reading in English
C. How to write an E-mail in English
D. Make English Learning Interesting

B

There is a pretty beautiful bird and she lives in a tall tree in the mountain. Every morning she gets up very early and sings. She sings very well. So when she sings, many animals come to listen to her. When she sings happily, you can get pearls(珍珠) from her mouth.

One day, a man knows that, he goes into the mountain and looks for it. After a few days, he finds her and gets her. Then he takes her home. But the bird is very sad, she doesn’t sing at all. The man thinks a lot of ways to let her sing. But it doesn’t work.

At last, he takes the bird to the king. The king is very happy and gives the bird to his daughter on her birthday.

The princess is a beautiful girl. She is very kind and let the bird fly away. The bird is very happy and sings for the princess. And the princess gets a lot of nice pearls.

From that day, the bird comes to meet the princess every day and sings for her.

1. Many animals come to the bird because she ______.
A. is beautiful　B. lives in a tall tree　C. is happy　D. sings very well
2. How does the man know people can get pearls from the bird’s mouth?
A. He sees it.　B. He is the bird’s friend.
C. Someone tells him.　D. We don’t know.
3. The man gets ______ from the bird.
A. pearls　B. songs　C. a princess　D. nothing
4. The word “princess” is ______ in Chinese.
A. 公主　B. 王子　C. 小姐　D. 千金
5. From the passage, we know ______.
A. the king likes the bird
B. the princess makes friends with the bird
C. the king makes friends with the man
D. the princess eats the bird
