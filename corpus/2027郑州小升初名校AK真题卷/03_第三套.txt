There is a little boy and he has a bad temper（脾气）. His father wants to change（改变）him. Then he gives his son a box of nails（钉子）. He tells his son to put a nail in the wall every time he loses his temper. The first day the boy puts 37 nails into the wall. It is hard for him to do that. And the second day he puts 20 nails into the wall. Gradually（逐渐地）the boy finds that it is easy to keep calm（平静的）, but difficult to put the nails into the wall. At last, the box is empty and the boy’s temper becomes

good. Then the father tells the son to pull out（拔出）the nails and says, “Look at the holes, although the nails were gone, the wall will never be the same as before.” Try to keep calm. Don’t hurt（伤害）others easily. Sometimes it’s not enough to say sorry when you do something wrong.
