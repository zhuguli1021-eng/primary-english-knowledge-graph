There’s a 7-Eleven close to my house. I love the slush(冰沙) drinks in the shop. They’re quite cheap.
The cups have different sizes. A small cup is only fifty cents. A large cup is one dollar(美元). A super cup
is two dollars. A mega(巨型的) cup is three dollars. I usually buy a large one. When it’s very hot and I feel
quite thirsty, I buy a super cup. The mega cup is too big. I never give it a go.
I get money for cleaning the floor every day after supper. If I do my job very well from Monday to Friday,
Mum gives me two dollars on Saturday. When I get my money, the first thing I do is buy a slush drink. What
kind of slush do I like best? The orange one. I have two dollars in my hand now, and I’ll go and get my slush.
