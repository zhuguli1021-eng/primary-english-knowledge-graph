### 六、宇航员在太空中是如何生活的呢？ （10 分）

Astronauts’ life in space is very different from life on Earth. They live in a small space station and follow a strict daily schedule.

In the morning, they can’t take a shower like we do. They clean their bodies with wipes and use special toothpaste that they have to “eat” it. For breakfast, they eat special space food in tubes or bags, because crumbs ( 碎屑 ) would float away.

During the day, they work hard. They do scientific experiments, fix the space station, and take photos of Earth. Every afternoon, they must exercise for at least two hours to keep healthy and strong, since there is no gravity ( 重力 ) in space.

At night, they sleep in small sleeping bags tied to the wall. They can see the Sun rise and set sixteen times a day. It is a hard but amazing life!

（　　）1. Astronauts can take a shower every morning in space.

### 七、请你用几句话介绍一下太阳。不少于 45 个单词。（10 分）

易错题辨析

句型转换。

There are some living things.（改为一般疑问句）

解析：本题考查 There be 句型的一般疑问句。There be 句型在变一般疑问句时，将 be 动词提至句首，句中有 some 的，需改为 any。

答案：Are there any living things?
