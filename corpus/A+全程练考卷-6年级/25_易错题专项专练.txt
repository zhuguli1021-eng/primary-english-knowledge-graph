### 十一、水很重要，地球上的淡水资源非常有限。我们要节约用水。(10 分)

Water is very important for all living things. We need water to drink, cook and clean every day. But now, clean water is becoming less and less. Many places do not have enough clean water. But some people even waste much water. They leave the water running when brushing teeth or washing hands.

It is time for us to save water. First, we should turn off the tap when we do not use water. Second, we can reuse water. For example, we can use vegetable washing water to water flowers. Third, we should take quick showers. If we save water now, we will have clean water in the future. Saving water is saving our Earth.
