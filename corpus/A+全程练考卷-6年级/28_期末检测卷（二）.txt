### 六、在过去的一年中，Tingting 一家趁着节假日游览了不少城市。(10 分)

I’m Tingting. My family went to many cities last year. Last Labour Day holiday, we went to Tianjin. We ate *Goubuli baozi*, watched *xiangsheng* shows. Last Dragon Boat Festival holiday, we went to Hangzhou. We walked around the West Lake. We drank *Longjing* tea, ate *Dongpo* pork. Last summer holidays, we went to Beijing. We climbed the Great Wall. We visited the Palace Museum, Tian’anmen Square and the Summer Palace. We also visited the Bird’s Nest. It was amazing. Last Mid-Autumn Festival holiday, we went to Shaoxing. We visited the former home of Lu Xun. Last National Day holiday, we went to Xi’an. We visited the Terracotta Warriors. We rode bikes on the old city wall. Last Spring Festival holiday, we went to Shanghai. The view from the tower was great. I enjoyed these trips.
