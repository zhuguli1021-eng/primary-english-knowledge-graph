### 六、孩子们该如何管理零花钱呢？ （10 分）

Pocket money is important for us, so we need to use it wisely. First, we should make a spending plan. We can list things we need and things we want. For example, we need to buy school things like

— 55 —

dictionaries, but we may want to buy toys or snacks.

Before buying anything, we should ask ourselves some questions: Do I need it now? Do I ready have one? Is it expensive? Do I have enough money? If something is expensive, we can wait for a sale to save money. We can also save some pocket money for big things, like a computer or a microscope.

It’s good to share our money with others too. We can buy nice gifts for our family. By making good plans, we can learn to save and spend money wisely.

### 七、假如你在商店看到一辆自行车，你特别喜欢，但你的钱不够，你会怎么做？请写一写，不少于 45 个单词。（10 分）

易错题辨析

单项选择。

（　　）He ______ a haircut.

A. is going to have　B. go to have　C. is going have　D. have

解析：本题考查 be going to 结构一般将来时的谓语形式。本题没有时间词，不能确定时态。主语 he 是第三人称单数，若时态为一般现在时，谓语动词用单三形式。四个选项中 B，D 符合一般现在时，但没有单三形式，故排除。A 项是 be going to + 动词原形，符合一般将来时的结构，故选 A。C 项中没有 to，不正确。

答案：A
