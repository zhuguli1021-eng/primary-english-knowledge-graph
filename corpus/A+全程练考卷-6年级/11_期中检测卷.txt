七、Liu Jia 一家上个暑假去了北京旅游。他们的旅程如何呢？ （10 分）

Last summer holidays, my parents and I went on a 3-day trip to Beijing. On the first day, we visited the Great Wall at Badaling. It was crowded but amazing—we walked along the ancient stones for two hours. In the evening, we went to the Bird’s Nest, the main stadium of the 2008 Olympic Games. We took many photos. The next day, we visited the Palace Museum. I was shocked by the red walls, golden roofs, and countless cultural relics there. On the last day, we had Beijing Roast Duck for lunch and then visited the Summer Palace. We rowed a boat on Kunming Lake and enjoyed the beautiful gardens. It was a wonderful trip, and I hope to visit Beijing again soon.
