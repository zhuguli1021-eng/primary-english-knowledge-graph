## 核心知识

单词

astronaut 宇航员；航天员　month 月；月份　question 问题　time 次；回　soil 土壤　alien 外星生物　sunrise 日出　rover 探测器　into 朝；进入　telescope 望远镜　spaceship 宇宙飞船　taikonaut 中国航天员　cloth(一块)布；织物　toothpaste 牙膏　sunset 日落　Mars 火星

句型

①—Is there any water on Mars?

火星上有水吗？

—Yes, there is. There is soil too.

是，有的。那里还有土壤。

②—Are there any aliens?

那儿有外星人吗？

—I don’t think so. They say there aren’t any living things.

我不这样认为。据说那里没有生物。

③ China already put a rover on Mars.

中国已经在火星上放置了探测器。

④ The taikonauts get up early in the morning.

中国航天员早上起床早。

### 八、人类怎样探索神秘的太空呢？ (15 分)

People always want to know about space. Now we can use many ways to explore it.

1. We use telescopes. They help us see far stars and planets clearly.

2. We send satellites into space. They take photos and send information back to Earth.

3. We also have spaceships. They carry astronauts into space. Astronauts do experiments (实验) and look for new things. Some spaceships go to other planets without people. They can travel for many years.
