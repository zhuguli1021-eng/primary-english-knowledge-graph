On May 24, 2026, the spaceship Shenzhou-23 took off from Jiuquan, a city in northwest China. A few hours later, it reached China's space station, Tiangong.
There were three astronauts on board. Zhu Yangzhu was the leader of the crew(乘组). It was his second time in space. Zhang Zhiyuan was the pilot, and this was his first trip. The third astronaut, Lai Ka-ying, made history. She is the first person from Hong Kong to travel to space.
Lai is a scientist. In the space station, she does experiments(实验) and looks after the science machines. Up there, nothing stays on the floor. People, pens and even water float in the air.
This mission(任务) is special in another way, too. Most astronauts stay in the station for about six months, but one of the crew will stay for about a year. No Chinese astronaut has stayed in space that long before. Doctors will check everyone's body and mind before they choose who will stay.
Lai's story shows that a dream can come true if you keep working hard for it.

Gongyi has a long history. It is famous for its old kilns(窑) and white porcelain(白瓷). Today, people there are working hard to pass old skills like the lion dance, wood carving(木雕) and porcelain making on to young people.
Chen Tingang is a wood carver. For years, Chen has taught wood carving in local schools. In these classes, students learn to carve, and they learn about history and art at the same time. "We pursue perfection in every detail," Chen says. That means they always try to make every small part perfect.
Not every old skill is easy to keep. Han Hongli, a porcelain maker, says young people need many years to learn this skill, and they can't make much money at first. So Han started a study base. There, students make porcelain with their own hands and learn about the culture behind it.
Old skills are like old friends. If no one visits them, they will be forgotten. Thanks to these teachers, more and more children in Gongyi are getting to know them.

%% 题材=科普跨学科 | 来源=原创（自然科学常识） | 标题=Why Do Leaves Change Colour?
In spring and summer, most leaves are green. Why? Leaves have something green in them. It is called chlorophyll(叶绿素). It helps the tree make food from sunlight, water and air.
In autumn, the days get shorter and colder. Trees get less sunlight, so they stop making food. The green chlorophyll slowly goes away. Then we can see other colours, like yellow and orange. These colours were in the leaves all the time, but the green colour covered them.
Some leaves turn red, too. This red colour is made in autumn, when there is a lot of sugar in the leaves and the nights are cool.
At last, the leaves fall to the ground. This helps trees save water in the cold winter. Next spring, new green leaves will grow again.
%%

Last Friday was our school sports day. I was in the 800-metre race. I had practised every morning for a month, and I really wanted to win.
When the race began, I ran fast and soon I was in second place. Then, in front of me, a girl from Class 3 fell down. She held her leg and couldn't get up. The other runners ran past her. I stopped. If I helped her, I would lose the race. But how could I just leave her there?
I helped her up and we walked to the finish line together. We came last. I felt a little sad, because all my hard work seemed to be for nothing.
But then something surprising happened. Everyone stood up and clapped for us. Our PE teacher came over and said, "Today you didn't win the race, but you won something more important." The girl smiled at me and said thank you.
On my way home, I thought about the teacher's words. Now I understand what he meant. I lost a race, but I found a friend.

The blue bin is for things we can use again, like paper, plastic bottles and glass. The green bin is for food waste(厨余垃圾), like vegetable leaves and fruit skins. The red bin is for harmful(有害的) things, like old batteries(电池) and medicine. The grey bin is for other rubbish.
