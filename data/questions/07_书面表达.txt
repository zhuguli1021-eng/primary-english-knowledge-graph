## [5/1] My Weekend
任务：周末生活丰富多彩。请以“My Weekend”为题，介绍你周末通常做些什么。
要求：不少于 40 词；用一般现在时；书写规范。
要点：周六做什么；周日做什么；你的感受
词数：40
好句：On Saturday morning, I usually... / In the afternoon, I often... / I like my weekend because...
范文：
I have a happy weekend. On Saturday morning, I usually do my homework. In the afternoon, I often play football with my friends in the park. On Sunday, I help my mother clean the house. Then we go shopping. In the evening, I read books. I like my weekend because it is busy and fun.

## [6/2] My School Day
任务：假如你叫林辉，请以“My School Day”为题，按照时间顺序介绍自己一天的活动安排。
要求：不少于 60 词，题目不计入总词数；语法正确，文章流畅；书写规范。
要点：起床和早饭；到校和上午的课；午饭；下午的课和放学后的活动；晚上的安排
词数：60
好句：I get up at... / First... Then... After that... / School is over at... / I go to bed at...
范文：
I'm Lin Hui. Let me tell you about my school day. I get up at 6:30 every morning. After breakfast, I go to school by bike. Classes begin at 8:00. We have four classes in the morning. My favourite class is science. At 12:00, I have lunch at school. We have three classes in the afternoon. School is over at 4:30. Then I play basketball with my classmates. I get home at 5:30 and do my homework. After dinner, I read for half an hour. I go to bed at 9:30. My school day is busy, but I enjoy it.

## [7/2] Then and Now
任务：同学们，你们即将迈入初中。和五年前相比，你有哪些变化？请以“Then and Now”为题写一篇短文。
要求：不少于 70 词；注意一般过去时和一般现在时的对比；书写规范。
要点：外貌的变化；性格的变化；爱好或能力的变化；你的感想
词数：70
好句：Five years ago, I was... Now I am... / I didn't like... but now I... / I have changed a lot.
范文：
Five years ago, I was a little girl. I was short and thin, and I had short hair. Now I am tall, and I have long hair. I was shy then. I didn't like to talk to others in class. But now I am outgoing, and I often answer questions in class. I didn't like reading before, but now I read a book every week. I could not swim then, but now I can swim very well. I have changed a lot, and I am ready for junior high school.

## [7/3] A Trip to Kaifeng
任务：假如你上个暑假和家人去了开封旅行。请以“A Trip to Kaifeng”为题，介绍这次旅行。
要求：不少于 80 词；用一般过去时；书写规范，条理清楚。
要点：什么时候、和谁去的；怎么去的；参观了哪些地方；吃了什么；感受
词数：80
好句：Last summer, I went to... with... / We went there by train. / First... Then... / It was the best trip I have ever had.
范文：
Last summer, I went to Kaifeng with my parents. We went there by train. It only took us about half an hour. First, we visited Qingming Riverside Park(清明上河园). The old buildings were beautiful, and we watched an interesting show there. Then we went to see the Iron Pagoda(铁塔). It was very tall and old. In the evening, we walked in a night market and tried soup dumplings. They were really delicious. I learned a lot about the history of Kaifeng. I felt tired but happy. I hope to visit it again.

## [7/3] A Reply to Mike
任务：假如你是李华，你的美国笔友 Mike 发来邮件，问你所在城市怎样进行垃圾分类。请给他回一封邮件。
要求：不少于 80 词；邮件格式正确（开头和结尾已给出，不计入词数时可不写）；书写规范。
要点：感谢来信；介绍四种垃圾桶和放什么；垃圾分类的好处；问问他那里的情况
词数：80
好句：Thanks for your email. / In my city, there are... / ...is for... / What about your city?
范文：
Dear Mike,
Thanks for your email. I'm glad to tell you about rubbish sorting(垃圾分类) in my city. There are four kinds of rubbish bins here. The blue bin is for paper, plastic bottles and glass, because we can use them again. The green bin is for food waste(厨余垃圾). The red bin is for harmful(有害的) things like old batteries(电池). The grey bin is for other rubbish. Sorting rubbish makes our city cleaner, and it helps us save things. Now my family sorts rubbish every day. What about your city? Please write back soon.
Yours,
Li Hua

## [7/3] An Old Skill in My Hometown
任务：学校英语社团举办“用英语讲中国故事”活动。请介绍你家乡河南的一项传统技艺（如剪纸、少林功夫、汴绣、皮影戏等）。题目自拟。
要求：不少于 80 词；说明它是什么、有什么特点、你对它的看法；书写规范。
要点：技艺名称和历史；怎么做或有什么特点；你的体验；为什么要传承
词数：80
好句：...has a long history. / It is famous for... / I tried it... / We should... so that...
范文：
Paper-cutting in My Hometown
I come from Henan. Paper-cutting is a traditional skill in my hometown, and it has a long history. People cut beautiful pictures out of red paper with scissors. Some pictures show animals, and some show flowers or Chinese characters. During the Spring Festival, people put paper-cuts on their windows for good luck. Last winter, I learned paper-cutting from my grandma. It was not easy, but I made a little red rabbit. I felt very proud. I think we should learn more about old skills like this, so that they will not be forgotten.
